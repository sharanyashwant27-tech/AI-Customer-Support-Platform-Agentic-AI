# Shipping FAQs

## How long does shipping take?

- Standard: 5–7 business days
- Express: 2–3 business days
- Overnight: next business day (order before 2pm local)

## Tracking

Once shipped, a tracking number is emailed and available via order lookup (`ORD-####`).

## International

Duties and taxes may apply and are the responsibility of the recipient unless otherwise stated at checkout.

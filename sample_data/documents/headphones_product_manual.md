# Headphones Pro — Product Manual

## Overview

Wireless Headphones Pro (SKU-WH-01) deliver ANC, 30-hour battery life, and Bluetooth 5.3.

## Setup

1. Charge for 2 hours before first use
2. Hold the power button for 3 seconds
3. Pair from your device Bluetooth menu as **WH-Pro**

## Controls

- Single tap: play / pause
- Double tap: next track
- Triple tap: previous track
- Hold 2 seconds: toggle noise cancelling

## Troubleshooting

If audio cuts out, reset by holding power + ANC for 10 seconds until the LED flashes amber.

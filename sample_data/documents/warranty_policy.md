# Warranty Policy

Hardware products include a **12-month limited warranty** against manufacturing defects.

## Covered

- Failure under normal use
- Battery capacity below 70% within 12 months (Headphones Pro)

## Not covered

- Accidental damage, liquid exposure, unauthorized repairs
- Consumables and gift cards

## Claims

Provide order ID and a short description of the defect. Approved claims receive repair, replacement, or store credit.

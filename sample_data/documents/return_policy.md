# Return & Refund Policy

Customers may return eligible products within **30 days** of delivery.

## Eligibility

- Item must be unused and in original packaging
- Proof of purchase (order ID) is required
- Digital goods and personalized items are non-refundable

## Process

1. Start a return from the Orders page or by chatting with support
2. Print the prepaid shipping label
3. Drop off the package within 7 days of label creation
4. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after inspection

## Exchanges

Exchanges are processed as a return + new order to ensure inventory accuracy.

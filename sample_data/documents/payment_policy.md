# Payment Policy

## Failed payments

- A failed card authorization does **not** capture funds.
- Customers may retry immediately with the same or another payment method.
- Ensure billing address and CVV match the card issuer records.
- After **two failed retries**, support may open a billing ticket and send a secure payment link.

## Receipts & double charges

- Successful payments appear on the statement within 1–2 business days.
- Suspected double charges should be reported with the order ID for reconciliation.

## Support next steps

1. Retry payment
2. Confirm card details
3. Create billing ticket if still failing
4. Escalate to billing specialist when fraud or legal language appears
